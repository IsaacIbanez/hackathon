# N3 measurements: document scanning (before and after)

Task N3 (Pau), brief in `docs/briefs/N3.md`. Tool: `scanner/benchmark/` (the photos and the results are not in the repo).

## How it was measured
- **24 photos with the MX Brio at 4K**, taken by Pau on 2026-11-22 with the camera test page: the 3 printed test papers (`scanner/pdf/samples/print/`) × 4 conditions × 2 photos.
  - **Conditions:**
    - normal (on a dark jacket and with the light floor beside it);
    - held in the hand (with people and chairs behind);
    - white table (with cables and a can beside it);
    - low light.
  - **In many photos the paper is upside down**, as a camera looking at the table from the screen sees it.
- **Correct words:** the words of the original PDF's body text (10 pt or more) that appear in the `document`, in any order. The grey 8–9 pt header and footer cannot be read at that distance and are counted separately, in «all the text».
- **Corners:** all 4 corners of the page found, and forming an A4 (±10 %).
- **Time:** the sum of the stages needed for the `document` (input, geometry, enhancement, orientation, quality and OCR) on Pau's PC. It does not include the photo.
- **«Before» and «after» are computed over the same 24 photos.**

- **All the text** (with the header and the footer): from 51 % to 63 %.
- **Per paper:**
  - 01, letter: 91 % → 90 %;

- **With the real service (`POST /scan`):**
  - it answers in 3.7–5.6 s with the `document`;

  - Before, the answer also waited for the digital PDF, which took 6.5 s on average (up to 30 s).
  - The white-table and hand-held photos gave **422 «no sheet found»**; now they return their text.

## What failed and what was changed (in Didac's pipeline, `scanner/pdf/`, and in `scanner/service.py`)
1. **Upside-down paper was not rotated** (the cause of almost all the 2–12 % results).
   - **Cause:** the orientation was decided with **a crop** of «the area with the most letters». With no page detected, that area was a can or a fleece. Also, with small grey text, Tesseract could not read any word confidently in any rotation.
   - **Now:**
     - Tesseract's orientation detector tries **several crops**;
     - if in doubt, **the whole page** is read, scaled to letters of ~28 px, upright and upside down (sideways only if neither of the two reads anything), and the one that reads the most words wins.
   - It got 9 out of 9 right in the hard photos (for example, 85 words against 8).
2. **A «found» page that was not the page** (dark table).
   - **Cause:** a quadrilateral that included half a jacket was chosen. When straightened, the text came out distorted: 6–8 % of words.
   - **Now:** a light quadrilateral with a large dark area inside (more than 15 %) is discarded. A page only has letters and thin lines. It does not apply to dark documents, like a poster or a screen.
   - Corners on a dark table: 1/6 → 6/6.
3. **With no page detected, the whole 4K photo was read** (white table and held in the hand).
   - **Cause:** the can and the cables were read, and the letters could not be enlarged because the photo was already at the size cap.
   - **Now:**
     - **the text blocks that sit on paper** are cropped (letters on a light background; fleece or hair do not count);
     - the resolution is estimated from the letters' height, so the OCR enlarges as much as needed;
     - if the image's edge is already paper (a scan, a photo already cropped), nothing is cropped.
   - The form on the white table went from 8–11 % to 91–93 %.
4. **The service answered 422 without reading** when it did not find the page.
   - **Now:** it only answers 422 if there is no text on paper either. If there is text, it returns the `document` without `pageCorners` (optional in the contract).
5. **The answer waited for the PDFs.**
   - **Now:**
     - the pipeline signals as soon as the `document` is ready (`Options.on_document`);
     - the service answers then and finishes the PDFs in the background;
     - the OCR lock is released at that moment, so the next photo does not wait.
   - **The digital PDF is on by default** (Pau's decision). It is turned off with `?digital=0` or `SCANNER_DIGITAL=0`.
   - New `X-Scan-Folder` header: the folder where the PDFs appear.

## What was not achieved (and why)
- **The brief's goals:**
  - **95 % in normal and 90 % in the rest, not reached** (72–83 %);
  - **≤ 5 s, yes** (4.3 s);
  - **corners 6/6 in normal, yes**; in the rest, no.
- **Blurry photos cannot be read** (normal-03-1, dim-02-1, white-03), not even by a person: sharpness of 52–115, against more than 1400 in the sharp ones.
  - They were taken with the test page's «Capture still», which does not wait for focus.
  - The app's framing guide (`startFramingGuide`, T19) does wait for it and warns aloud, so in the app they would not have been taken.
  - **Next step:** repeat the photos with the guide and measure again.
- **White table and held in the hand: 0/6 and 2/6 corners.** Between the page and the white table there are only 13–16 grey levels (the detector asks for 25), and in the hand the fingers cover the edge.
  - The text is read anyway with the text-block crop, but without corners the pointer cannot point at the paper.
  - Lowering the thresholds would cause «ghost» pages; it remains pending.
- **Small losses** from the text-block crop: hand-01 94→90 % and 92→86 %; white-01-2 95→89 %; white-03-2 36→26 %.
- **Tests:** the pipeline's 81 tests pass, and so do the capture and connector ones.
  - `test_service.test_scanned_pdf_goes_through_ocr` already failed before these changes: `pypdfium2` is missing from the environment that `install.ps1` creates.
  - `test_health_and_unknown_path` failed 1 time out of 12 runs and could not be repeated: it could be a flaky test with the PDFs in the background.

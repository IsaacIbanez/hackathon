# N8. Show that the above is optimal (efficiency)

Owner: unassigned · Depends on: N7 · Board: `TAREAS.md` · How to start: `docs/TRABAJAR.md`.

## Goal
Show that the above is optimal (efficiency): measurable numbers for cost, latency and tokens, compared with a naive alternative, so they can be shown in the demo. It is a scoring criterion of the AXA challenge.

## Current status
**Branches:** since #44 (2026-11-22), `main` holds the integration (#38), the per-person startup (#39), guided reading (#40) and the overlay app. What is cited from `feature/T6-camera-controls` (#41, Nil) is not in `main` yet: check whether it has been merged already.

- **Meter** (`backend/llm`, `Meter`): per tier (cheap or expensive) it counts calls, cache hits, errors, cost in USD, and the average and last latency.
  - It reaches the interface in the `stats` message and is shown in `web/src/app/Cost.tsx` (previous screen).
  - **Tokens** are in each response (`llm.Response.PromptTokens` and `CompletionTokens`), **but not in `stats`.**
- **Figures measured before the integration:**
  - in `docs/PITCH.md`: explaining with Haiku took 1.8 s and $0.00012;
  - in `docs/VOZ.md`: the transcription.
- **Parts that cost nothing:** reading the pointed block, the framing notices and guided reading (local voice).
- **There is no comparison with a naive alternative** (for example, always sending the whole photo to a large model).

## Open questions

- How it is shown in the demo: a panel in the app, a slide or both.

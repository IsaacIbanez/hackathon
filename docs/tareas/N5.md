# N5. Cursor mode

Owner: unassigned · Depends on: N1, N3, N4 · Board: `TAREAS.md` · How to start: `docs/TRABAJAR.md`.

## Goal
Cursor mode. Once the document is finished, it is reachable from cursor mode. From that mode you can start reading and do actions.

## Current status
**Branches:** since #44 (2026-11-22), `main` holds the integration (#38), the per-person startup (#39), guided reading (#40) and the overlay app. What is cited from `feature/T6-camera-controls` (#41, Nil) is not in `main` yet: check whether it has been merged already.

- **Gesture Control with #41** (Nil, branch `feature/T6-camera-controls`, open PR, based on #29):
  - the index finger moves the **system cursor** (`SetCursorPos` in `adapters/desktop/desktop_windows.go`);
  - joining the middle finger and the thumb clicks, two quick taps right-click and holding drags;
  - menu with the fist ✊;
  - volume and brightness with the pinch.
- **In the app:**
  - pointer over the document: `web/src/app/pointing.ts` and `bridge.ts`;
  - reading of the pointed block in Go: `backend/session`.
- **There is nothing called «cursor mode»** nor a step from «finished document» to another mode.

## «Done» criteria (draft, to be confirmed with the person)
- From a finished document you enter cursor mode in the agreed way.
- From there, reading and the agreed actions are started, tested with a real hand or with the mouse as decided.
- You leave without leaving processes or the camera open.

# N3. Perfect the document scan

Owner: Pau · Depends on: — · Board: `TAREAS.md` · How to start: `docs/TRABAJAR.md`.

## Goal
Perfect the document scan.

## Current status
**Branches:** since #44 (2026-11-22), `main` holds the integration (#38), the per-person startup (#39), guided reading (#40) and the overlay app. What is cited from `feature/T6-camera-controls` (#41, Nil) is not in `main` yet: check whether it has been merged already.

- **Python pipeline** (Didac, #26 and #34, already in the integration): `scanner/pdf/` (`scanner_pdf`) and `scanner/service.py` (`POST /scan`, 127.0.0.1:8770).
  - It does: page detection, perspective, orientation, lighting, OCR with Tesseract (psm 3 and 6 passes in parallel), a searchable PDF, and a `document` with `pageCorners`.
  - Its own measurements in `scanner/ENTREGA.md`: scan from 10.5 to 8 s; orientation 12 out of 12; A4 letter in 4K.
  - One page per call.
- **Capture in the app** (Pau, #31 and T19): `web/src/camera/`, with `openCamera`, `captureStill` and `startFramingGuide` (spoken framing guide and automatic shot).
- **In the overlay:** `web/src/app/overlay/DocumentsMode.tsx` joins the camera, the guide and the `ReadPhoto` binding, which calls the service, and then `GuidedReader`.
- **Test documents:** 3 printable PDFs with made-up data in `scanner/pdf/samples/print/`.
- **Verified in the integration** (`docs/historial/ESTADO_PROYECTO.md`, E4, on that branch): the service starts and answers 422 without a page. **Not verified with real paper:** there was no Tesseract.

## Open questions
For the person who picks up the task; they cannot be answered by reading the repo.
- What fails today, specifically: quality, speed, corners, recognised text, several pages, lighting, glare, white paper on a white table…
- Which camera it is measured with: the MX Brio or the laptop's.
- A measurable goal: percentage of correct words in the test PDFs, seconds from the photo to hearing it, percentage of pages cropped right.
- Capture-to-PDF flow: is the PDF needed or is the `document` enough to read it? Several pages per document?
- Is the digital PDF with the vision model turned on (`?digital=1`, paid) or does it stay off?

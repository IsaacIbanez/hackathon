# N9. Voice control of the volume

Owner: unassigned · Depends on: — (related to N4) · Board: `TAREAS.md` · How to start: `docs/TRABAJAR.md`.

## Goal
Voice control of the volume: being able to turn the volume down by voice.

## Current status
**Branches:** since #44 (2026-11-22), `main` holds the integration (#38), the per-person startup (#39), guided reading (#40) and the overlay app. What is cited from `feature/T6-camera-controls` (#41, Nil) is not in `main` yet: check whether it has been merged already.

- **Part of it already exists**, by Nil, in #29 inside the integration:
  - `web/src/input/voice/commands.ts` recognises, in Spanish and English, «más alto», «sube», «sube el volumen»… (louder, up, volume up) and «más bajo», «baja», «baja el volumen»… (quieter, down, volume down);
  - `voiceInput.ts` raises or lowers the **volume of the app's voice** (`Speaker`) in steps of 0.2.
- **It only listens when asked to** (push to talk or holding the finger): there is no continuous listening.
- **System volume:** in #41 (`feature/T6-camera-controls`), Gesture Control changes it with the pinch, with a per-system implementation in `adapters/desktop/desktop_*.go`.
- Not verified with a microphone by the person writing this.

## Open questions
For the person who picks up the task; they cannot be answered by reading the repo.
- Which volume is controlled: the app's own voice (already partly exists) or the system's.
- Which commands and in which languages (today, Spanish and English).
- Whether it should also raise and mute.
- How listening is turned on: push, wake word, continuous listening (consumption and privacy).
- How it is confirmed: a beep, a sentence or the level on screen.

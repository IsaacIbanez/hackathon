# Brief N3: perfect the document scan
Person: Pau (P4). Confirmed by Pau on 2026-11-22. Sheet: `docs/tareas/N3.md`.

## Goal
That a paper page held in front of the MX Brio is read well, fast and with its corners found correctly, also in hard conditions. First we **measure**, then we **fix the worst** and at the end we **measure again**.

## Current status (read from the repo)
- **Didac's pipeline:** `scanner/pdf/` (`scanner_pdf`) and `scanner/service.py`, with `POST /scan` on 8770.
  - Page detection, perspective, orientation, lighting, OCR with Tesseract (psm 3 and 6 in parallel), a searchable PDF and a `document` with `pageCorners`.
- **Capture:**
  - in the app, `web/src/camera/` (`openCamera`, `captureStill`, `startFramingGuide`);
  - in Python, `scanner/capturar.py` (MX Brio at 4K).
- **Overlay:** `web/src/app/overlay/DocumentsMode.tsx` joins the camera, the guide and the reading service.
- **The digital PDF (`?digital=1`, with a vision model) exists but nobody asks for it:**
  - Go does not pass that parameter;
  - also, it is generated *before* returning the `document`, so it would delay the reading.
- **It has never been measured with real paper.** Pau's PC had no Tesseract; it is installed with `scanner/pdf/scripts/install.ps1`.
- **Test documents:** 3 printable PDFs with made-up data in `scanner/pdf/samples/print/` (01 a letter in Spanish, 02 a claim form, 03 a notice in Catalan). They have real text, which serves as the reference for the measurement.

## Pau's decisions
- Measure first and fix afterwards.
- With the **MX Brio**.
- Goals: **correct words**, **seconds from the photo until it starts reading** and **pages cropped right**.
- Output: **`document` + searchable PDF + digital PDF** (on).
- **One page** per document.
- **Four conditions:**
  - normal (dark table, good light);
  - held in the hand;
  - white table;
  - low light or glare.

## Scope
1. **Prepare:**
   - install Tesseract;
   - print the 3 PDFs;
   - the MX Brio connected;
   - the OpenRouter key in `.env` (Isaac's; never in the repo).
2. **Measuring tool**, in `scanner/benchmark/` (new):
   - takes the photo with the Brio (`capturar.py`) and sends it to the service (`POST /scan`);
   - compares the text read with the real text of the original PDF (percentage of correct words);
   - checks the corners and saves an image with the page marked, to review it by eye;
   - measures the time per stage (photo, reading, PDF, digital PDF);
   - the photos and the results stay out of git.
3. **Measurement rounds:** 4 conditions × 3 papers × 2 photos (24 readings), before and after the fixes.
4. **Fix** whatever comes out worst:
   - in the capture (Pau);
   - in the pipeline (Didac, with their approval).
5. **Output:**
   - the `document` as soon as possible, so that reading starts right away;
   - the searchable PDF and the digital PDF, in the background, without delaying it.
6. **Before and after results** in `docs/medidas/N3.md`.

## Out of scope
- Several pages per document.
- New languages.
- Changing the framing guide.

## «Done» criteria (measurable)
| Condition | Correct words | Pages cropped right (all 4 corners) |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | ≥ 95 % | 6/6 |
| Held in the hand | ≥ 90 % | ≥ 5/6 |
| White table | ≥ 90 % | ≥ 5/6 |
| Low light or glare | ≥ 90 % | ≥ 5/6 |

- **From the photo until it starts reading:** ≤ 5 s in normal conditions, on Pau's PC. The PDFs do not delay it.
- **Searchable PDF and digital PDF** generated in every reading with a page.
- **Scanner tests** (`pytest` and `unittest`) green.
- Before and after measurements written down in `docs/medidas/N3.md`.

## Folders it touches
- **Own:** `web/src/camera/`, `scanner/capturar.py` and `scanner/benchmark/` (new).
- **Other people's, with their agreement:**
  - **Didac:** `scanner/pdf/` and `scanner/service.py` (the pipeline fixes and the PDFs in the background).
  - **Isaac:** asking for the digital PDF from Go (`backend/scanner`), if needed.

## Contract (`api/openapi.yaml`)
Does not change: `document` already carries `pageCorners`. The PDF routes go in the service's headers (`X-Scan-Pdf`), outside the contract.

## Models and cost
- **OCR:** local, $0.
- **Digital PDF:** Haiku through OpenRouter (`scanner/pdf/.../vision.py`), about $0.002 per page, about $0.05 for all the rounds. Its cost does not show up in the app's meter (a known pending item, `docs/historial/ESTADO_PROYECTO.md` §6.7).

## If it fails
- **Without Tesseract:** the service answers 503 and the app says so.
- **Without an OpenRouter key:** no digital PDF, but the `document` and the searchable PDF carry on.
- **No page in the photo:** 422, «no sheet found», and the framing guide asks to repeat it.

## Dependencies on other people
- **Didac:** approval to touch the pipeline and the service; how each part works.
- **Isaac:** the OpenRouter key, and the Go line that asks for the digital PDF if needed.

## Risks
- Tesseract is installed separately on each machine.
- Only one program at a time can use the Brio: the measurements are made with the overlay and the gestures closed.
- A printer is needed.
- The time left in the hackathon.

## Open questions
- Where the user sees the PDFs (folder, button in the overlay): decided with Isaac and Didac.

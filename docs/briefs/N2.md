# Brief N2: Keypad

Confirmed by Isaac on 2026-11-22. Task sheet: `docs/tareas/N2.md`.

## Goal
The **MX Keypad** (MX Creative Console) controls the app **without installing anything**: the app reads its keys directly over USB. The global shortcuts **Ctrl+Alt+Shift + key** stay as a fallback (Logi Options+ or a normal keyboard). **It works even when another window has the focus.**

- **Connection:**
  - direct reading of the keypad over USB, without Logi Options+ or an SDK;
  - the keyboard shortcuts, as a fallback.

## Scope (keypad page 1)

## Out of scope
- The Logitech SDK.
- The keypad connected over Bluetooth or through the Bolt receiver (another USB identifier): untested; the fallback shortcuts work there.
- Custom icons or text on the key screens.
- Linux and macOS.

## How
- **Direct reading** (`backend/overlay/keypadhid*.go`):
  - the app opens the keypad (Logitech, VID 046D, PID C354) as a HID device and reads its reports;
  - verified format: `13 ff 02 00 <counter> 01 <key>`, with the key from 1 to 9 on press and `00` on release;
  - if it is unplugged, the app looks for it again.
- **`backend/overlay`:** from one global shortcut to a **shortcut → action table**, registered on Windows with `RegisterHotKey`. If a shortcut is taken by another program, it is logged and the app carries on.
- **`app_overlay.go`:** when a shortcut is pressed, it shows the window if it is hidden and emits a Wails event with the action.
- **`web/src/app/overlay/keypad.ts`:** translates each action into the key that the menu and the reader already understand (`Esc`, `1`–`3`, `Space`, `←`, `→`, `R`). The reader's code (Didac) and the hand module's (Nil) **are not touched**.
- **`docs/KEYPAD.md`:** the table and how to set it up in Logi Options+.

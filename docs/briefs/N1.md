# Brief N1: solid overlay (Nil)

Confirmed by Nil on 2026-11-22. Sheet: `docs/tareas/N1.md`.

## Goal
That the overlay can be used entirely with the mouse and that it always has a visible camera at the top left with the hand points.

## Current status (read in `main`, commit d49e067)
- Gesture Control already lives inside the overlay (a single executable, decision of 2026-11-22): option 1 mounts the camera page (`mountGestureControl`) and talks to Go through `GestureCall`.
- The camera with the hand is only visible inside option 1, or in the bubble in the bottom corner.
- Buttons that already exist: choose an option, «← Menu», «Quit the app». Keyboard only: hide the overlay (Esc), the development screen (D) and the bubble (M; option 1 also has a button).
- Option 1 already has, with the mouse: turning the gestures on, Call/Computer, the call app and cursor mode.

## Scope
1. **Fixed camera at the top left, on every overlay screen:**
   - a small panel with the image and only the skeleton of the tracked hand (21 points), no text;
   - a button to hide and show it;
   - the same camera and the same detector as the gestures: the gesture engine becomes always on in the overlay;
   - it pauses while option 2 (read paper) uses the camera and comes back when you leave.
2. **Without a camera or with the camera busy:** a clear message in the panel; the rest of the app keeps working.
3. **Mouse-only navigation:** visible buttons to hide the overlay and for the development screen, besides the ones already there.
4. **Gesture controls with the mouse:** gestures on/off, cursor mode, bubble and Call/Computer, with visible buttons.
5. **A short guide in `docs/OVERLAY.md`:** «how to add a button or a panel», tested with a sample panel.

## Out of scope
- The screens of options 2, 3 and 4 (Pau and Didac).
- The face points.
- The keypad (N2).
- The global shortcut on Linux and macOS.

## «Done» criteria
- The whole overlay can be gone through without a keyboard: menu, each option, back, hide, quit and the gesture controls.
- The camera shows at the top left on every screen, with the hand live.
- Without a camera, a message, and the app stays usable.
- The guide exists and the sample panel works.
- Tests, lint, the web build and `go vet` green. `wails build` is tested by Nil on Windows.

## Folders I touch
- `web/src/input/` (Nil).
- `web/src/app/overlay/` (`Overlay.tsx`, `GesturesMode.tsx`, a new panel and a minimal change in `DocumentsMode.tsx` to pause the camera) and `docs/OVERLAY.md`. They are Isaac's and Didac's: they must be told.

## Contract
Does not change.

## Risks
- **A single camera:** it pauses for option 2. If Pau opens the camera on their own, it has to be coordinated.
- **Performance:** the detector is always on while the overlay is open.
